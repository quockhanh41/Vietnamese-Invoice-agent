## Footnote — flag false-positive rate, both ways

Raw combined false-positive rate (near_duplicate + price_outlier vs. `expected_finding_codes`, as-is): **2.807%** (8 of 285 clean invoices).

All 6 of near_duplicate's raw false positives are exactly the six injection negative-control twins (INV-0005, INV-0007, INV-0009, INV-0011, INV-0013, INV-0015) — `seed_injection`/`strip_injection` (scripts/generator/defects.py) clone one base spec per pair, so each twin shares `seller_tax_code`, `total_amount` and `issue_date` with its carrier and differs only in `invoice_number`, tripping `near_duplicate` at every window by construction (design §9.1/§9.3; task-5-report.md). They are not evidence about near_duplicate's calibration.

Attributing those six (excluded, not silently absorbed): **0.702%** (2 of 285 clean invoices). Residual false positives: INV-0016, INV-0168 (both `price_outlier` — `anomaly.PRICE_OUTLIER_Z`/`PRICE_OUTLIER_MIN_INVOICES`'s own documented floor, src/invoice_agent/domain/anomaly.py).

Both numbers are reported because the raw rate is what a naive scorer publishes and reads worse than the detector's actual behaviour, while silently excluding the six would hide a real, if benign, corpus-construction artifact. The table above uses the RAW (more conservative) rate for its verdict.
